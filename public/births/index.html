TFR from 1965 to present, with live 2026 birth counts as statistical offices release them. Gulf states show nationals-only figures — the only demographically meaningful measure where large expat majorities otherwise distort the picture.

Sources & methodology. TFR 1965–2025: WPP 2024 baseline (Demoria Research DHI). Gulf nationals: NSO series from FCSC (UAE), GASTAT (Saudi Arabia), NCSI (Oman) — confirmed anchor years, extended to 1965 via expat-share correction ramp. est~ = nationals series estimated beyond confirmed anchor (Kuwait post-2018, Qatar post-2019). calc~ = back-calculated from total TFR, census nationals-share, and assumed expat TFR 1.20 (Bahrain). Birth counts 2026: partial-year NSO provisional releases.
